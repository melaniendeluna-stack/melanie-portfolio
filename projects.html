Microbiology · Antibiotic Discovery

Tiny Earth

A semester-long research project focused on isolating soil bacteria and screening them for potential antimicrobial activity.

Purpose

The purpose of this project was to investigate soil microorganisms as possible sources of antibiotic-producing bacteria.

What I Did

I processed a soil sample, isolated bacterial colonies, completed pick-and-patch testing, performed tester overlays, and evaluated zones of inhibition.

Results

Several isolates demonstrated antimicrobial activity. Genomic analysis later supported the identification of an antibiotic-producing isolate.

Aseptic Technique Bacterial Isolation Pick-and-Patch Tester Overlay Zone Measurement

Molecular Biology · Gene Cloning

GAPDH Cloning

A molecular biology project involving the amplification and cloning of the carrot GAPDH gene.

Purpose

The project was designed to extract plant DNA, amplify the target gene, and introduce the amplified sequence into bacterial cells.

What I Did

I participated in DNA extraction, two rounds of PCR, gel electrophoresis, cloning, bacterial transformation, and evaluation of transformed colonies.

Results

PCR products were visualized through gel electrophoresis, and transformed bacterial colonies were produced following the cloning procedure.

DNA Extraction PCR Gel Electrophoresis Cloning Transformation

Genetics · Model Organisms

Drosophila Genetics

A genetics laboratory project using fruit flies as a model organism to investigate inheritance and observable traits.

Purpose

The project introduced the use of model organisms for studying inheritance, phenotype, and experimental genetics.

What I Did

I prepared fruit-fly media and agar, anesthetized specimens, handled cultures, and observed visible characteristics.

Skills Developed

The project strengthened my skills in specimen handling, culture preparation, careful observation, and laboratory documentation.

Model Organisms Media Preparation Anesthesia Microscopy Phenotype Observation

Bioinformatics · Bacterial Genomics

KBase Genome Analysis

A computational analysis of a bacterial isolate using genomic assembly, comparison, and identification tools.

Purpose

The analysis was used to investigate the identity and genomic characteristics of a bacterial isolate obtained during the Tiny Earth project.

What I Did

I used BLAST, genome-assembly tools, fastANI, and KBase workflows to compare genomic data and examine the organism’s possible identity and biological characteristics.

Skills Developed

This project introduced me to bacterial genomics, bioinformatics workflows, sequence comparison, and interpretation of computational results.

KBase BLAST fastANI Genome Assembly Bioinformatics
